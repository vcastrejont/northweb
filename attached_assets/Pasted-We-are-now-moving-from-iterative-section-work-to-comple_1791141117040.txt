# GLOBAL DESIGN DIRECTION

The complete homepage should feel like one coherent editorial experience.

Use:

- Northweb orange #F26522
- Northweb green #4CAF50 sparingly
- Charcoal #2F3E46
- Slate #6B7884
- Gray #E9EEF2
- White #FFFFFF

Typography:

Inter:
UI, navigation, body copy

Merriweather:
Headlines and editorial emphasis

Maintain the existing design language.

Avoid:

- excessive rounded cards
- excessive gradients
- glassmorphism
- generic SaaS layouts
- fake statistics
- fake client claims
- unnecessary animation
- excessive use of green
- decorative elements that don't serve the content

Prefer:

- strong typography
- whitespace
- editorial composition
- subtle borders
- restrained shadows
- large visual areas
- clear hierarchy
- orange used intentionally
